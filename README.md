# Custom Shopify development

Shopify developer in Austin, Texas, and founder of [Braun Design Co](https://braundesign.co).

I build custom Shopify storefronts for ecommerce brands, with a focus on platform migrations, front-end performance, and AI search optimization. Most of my work lives in Liquid, GraphQL, custom web pixels, and Shopify Flow, written to fit existing theme architecture rather than sit on top of it.

Right now I'm focused on AI Search Optimization (AEO and AISO) and agentic storefronts, helping brands stay discoverable as shoppers and AI agents move to AI-driven search.
